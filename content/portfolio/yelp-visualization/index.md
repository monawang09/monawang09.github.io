Selecting the right restaurant is often a frustrating experience. In this visualization, I aim to answer one of the most common questions people have when searching for places to eat:

What are the hidden gem restaurants near my current location?

This tool allows you to simulate being in California  and explore nearby neighborhoods to discover restaurants that are highly rated with more credible reviews and affordable price.
